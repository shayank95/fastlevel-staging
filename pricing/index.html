Ask about automation

Questions owners ask.

What does the Marketing Foundation cost?

US$3,999 one-time, with the scope agreed in writing. Third-party costs such as a domain, hosting or software subscriptions are separate and listed before you accept.

When do the 14 days start?

Once the kickoff requirements are agreed: scope, information, access and approvals. If a scope cannot fit 14 days, we tell you before you commit.

Who owns the website, data and accounts?

You do. Everything is set up in accounts in your business name, every login is documented, and it is all handed over.

Do I have to buy monthly services afterwards?

No. Your foundation never depends on ongoing services. Hire us, hire another provider, or run it yourself.

How do the bundles save money?

Simple arithmetic at today's prices. Growth Bundle: paid ads and SEO cost US$2,000 a month separately, US$1,500 together. Video Growth and Search & Video bundles: each pair starts at US$2,500 separately, US$2,000 together. Complete Growth Bundle: all four services start at US$4,000 separately, US$3,000 together. No former prices, no invented discounts.

Which bundles include the 30-Day Promise?

Standalone paid ads and the Growth Bundle. The Search & Video Bundle has no paid ads, so it has no lead promise. The Video Growth and Complete Growth bundles do not currently include the 30-Day Promise.

How does the paid ads performance guarantee work?

After a feasibility review we agree a qualified-lead target for your first 30 days, in writing. Your first month is paid upfront. If we miss the target, you pay a reduced fee until a 30-day period meets it: US$0 for standalone paid ads, US$750 per month on the Growth Bundle. Every service continues in full, normal billing resumes at the next cycle, and nothing is charged back. The promise covers paid ads results only, and ad spend stays yours.

Can you guarantee Google rankings?

No one can, and we never will. SEO is long-term work: technical fixes, key pages, your Google Business Profile and monthly reporting.

What is Your Own AI Influencer?

A custom AI spokesperson for your business, with a consistent look, voice and personality, who appears in video ads for your products, services and offers. It is not an influencer with an existing audience, and it does not guarantee sales. Ads are labeled as AI-generated where platforms require it.

How many videos do I get?

It depends on your goals and budget. Video volume, revisions and usage are agreed in writing before you buy, so you know exactly what you get each month. We never promise unlimited videos.

What support is included?

Support is included with every monthly service. Send requests any time, 24/7. We monitor the critical systems we manage and handle incidents by priority under your agreement.

What other costs should I expect?

Ad budgets go directly to Google or Meta. Email platforms and other third-party tools are billed separately and agreed before anything starts.

Can you automate our workflows too?

Yes. Custom workflow automation is a separate one-time project, quoted for your needs.
