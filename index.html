Frequently asked questions

Everything most business owners ask before getting started.

What is included in the $3,999 setup?

The setup gives you a complete marketing foundation: a website, lead capture, CRM, automated follow-ups, smart workflows, and tracking. It is built in accounts you own, so you are not locked into us.

Do I need a monthly subscription after the setup?

No. The $3,999 setup is a one-time build. After that, you only pay for ongoing services if you want them. There is no required monthly agency subscription just to keep your setup.

Who owns the website, CRM, and accounts?

You do. Your website, systems, and accounts belong to you. We build everything in assets you control so your business is never dependent on us to keep operating.

How long does the setup take?

Our target is 14 days once we have the required information, access, scope, and approvals. That gets you to a working marketing foundation faster, without dragging the project out for months.

What monthly services can I add after the setup?

You can choose individual services or save with a bundle. Individual services: Paid Advertising ($1,000/month), SEO ($1,000/month), Email Marketing ($500/month), and Your Own AI Influencer (starting at $1,500/month). Bundles: Growth Bundle (Paid Ads + SEO): $1,500/month, save $500. Video Growth Bundle (Paid Ads + AI Influencer): $2,000/month, save $500. Search & Video Bundle (SEO + AI Influencer): $2,000/month, save $500. Complete Growth Bundle (all four services): $3,000/month, save $1,000. Choose only what your business needs. Ad spend and applicable third-party costs are separate.

What does the 30-day paid ads guarantee mean?

For standalone paid ads management, we agree on a qualified-lead target. If that target is missed, management fees are waived until it is met. Ad spend is separate and paid directly to the ad platform.
